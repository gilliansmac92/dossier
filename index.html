Gillian Macdonald: Digital Dossier for Hire

The Digital Dossier

First and foremost I am a historian, and like other historians, I'm interested in uncovering stories about the past. As a digital historian, I use digital tools and methods to analyze and present historical data in innovative ways.

As the Director of the Lab for the Education and Advancement in Digital Research (LEADR)—one of the leading digital pedagogy labs in the country—I am committed to building digital public projects with digital methods and historical design at the forefront with diverse partners. My research output and agenda are driven by two interwoven threads: the production of analytical digital history projects using digital tools alongside traditional historical research, and digital public engagement projects in collaboration with both academic and non-academic partners. My goal is to make history and the skills associated with being a historian available to everyone and show applicability beyond just the classroom.This dossier
