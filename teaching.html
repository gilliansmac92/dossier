Past Achievements + Ongoing Projects

My research collaborations have been supported by $257,000 in private external funding and $33,500 in internal funding initiatives. This includes two major collaborative projects and an analytical digital history project.

My commitment to publicly engaged scholarship extends this research beyond academic audiences. I led the development of a major digital and physical museum exhibition Abundant Waters on Michigan’s and Michiganders' relationship with the water. I also have an ongoing digital project that is digitizing and making available for public consumption the Radical Historians Newsletter in collaboration with H-Net: Humanities and Social Sciences Online and the Society for United States Intellectual History (S-USIH). My ongoing projects including a collaboration with MATRIX, Center for Mark Twain Studies, and Stephen Rachman at MSU on the daily life of Mark Twain and his networks, and an MSU Tetrad funded initiative A Search For Research that helps scholars access archival materials in Russia and Ukraine during the ongoing war, addressing urgent needs in the scholarly community while navigating the politics of conflict. These projects demonstrate my sustained commitment to combining rigorous historical research with digital methods and public engagement, making scholarly knowledge accessible while addressing real-world challenges. My in-progress and future work extend the two strands of my research goals, I have partnered with a colleague Thomas Summerhill and H-Net to build a repository and digital project on Michigan's revolutionary past for the 250th anniversary of the Declaration of Independence and the American Revolution. The grant application will be submitted January 15th. In the near future I will also be working with my colleague Jim Ambuske to build what we have tentatively called "ScotHub" which will be a resource for all scholars of Scotland (far and wide) to be able to access content, resources, colleagues, and much more on H-Net Spaces.
